Biografi

Orville Swan Caesar ble født den 22 mai 1892 på/i Rice Lake, Barron County, Wisconsin, USA. Han giftet seg med Altie Lucille Peterson den 25 oktober 1913 på/i USA. Han døde den 19 mai 1965 på/i Sherman hospital, Elgin, Kane, Illinois, USA. Han ble gravlagt på/i Evergreen Cemetery, Barrington, Cook County, Illinois, USA.
Orville Swan Caesar had reference number 8590. He invented the Tropic-Aire hot-water heater, which replaced the dangerous and smelly exhaust-pipe system used for heating buses and later saw it become the standard for passenger cars.

During his teen years, he worked in an auto-repair shop. He later started a small bus service by operating a taxi fleet in Superior, Wisconsin.

In 1925 he teamed with Carl Eric Wickman, who had been building up a bus system in Minnesota since 1914.

With cash from stock sales and the foresight to see that bus routes could take over unprofitable train runs and serve as feeder lines, Caesar and Wickman began buying, merging and consolodating bus lines all over the country, making the Greyhound corporation what it is today.

Doris grew up in Los Angeles, CA. Later she moved to Chicago where she attended Northwestern University. While at Northwestern she met her future husband, Lyman W. Smith, MD. Doris and Lyman spent most of their lives in Barrington.
Early in her adult life Doris developed a passion for the art and design world. As a daughter of Orville Caesar, an inventor and co-founder of the Greyhound Corporation, she had the opportunity to travel extensively and discovered her innate talent and love for interior design.
