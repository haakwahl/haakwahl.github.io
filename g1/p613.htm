Alma Margaret Thorstad was also known as Alma Margaret Kvistero. She was baptized and confirmed at Vang Lutheran Church and attended country school in Dunn County.

After Melvin passed away, Alma returned to Sacred Heart where she continued making her wonderful Sunday dinners and her famous doughnuts. She moved to Henry Hill apartments and later entered the Granite Falls Manor.

Alma was an active member of Hawk Creek Lutheran Church and enjoyed being part of WELCA. She was a caregiver most of her life, and continued taking care of "old people" until she was over 80 herself. She accepted the limitations that came with advancing age with realistic dignity, and with gratitude for what was done for her. "Oh, I have it so good here," was her constant description of life at the Granite Falls Manor
