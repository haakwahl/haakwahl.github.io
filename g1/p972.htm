MorStella Helen Mathison (f. 21 juli 1924, d. 30 november 1989)

Biografi

Connie Corine Gusea ble født den 5 november 1946 på/i Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming, USA. Hun døde den 28 april 2012 på/i Cheyenne Regional Medical Center, Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming, USA. Donated to Medical Science.
Connie is survived by her life partner, Mike Creathbaum; a brother, Sam Gusea; daughter, Rhonda; two granddaughters, Courtney and Mackenna; sister-in-law Besty (Tal) Gusea-Gerringer; and numerous cousins, nieces and nephews.
Connie Corine Gusea was also known as Capps. Connie packed a tremendous amount of well-lived life into her lifetime, including a 20-year career as a flight attendant for Frontier Airlines where she was awarded the National Air Safety Award by the Association of Flight Attendants in 1979. The award is given annually to the flight attendant who contributed the most to the airline industry in the area of inflight safety.

In her second career, Connie was a Certified Traumatic Stress Specialist. During her years as a trauma worker, she was called to a number of man-made and natural disasters including the 1987 Continental Airlines #1713 crash in Denver, Colorado; the 1989 earthquake in Oakland, California; the 1989 United Airlines #232 crash in Sioux City, Iowa; the 1999 shootings at Columbine High School, and finally, three months at Ground Zero in New York City after 9/11. It was work that she loved, but it took a huge toll on her, both physically and emotionally. Hun had reference number 2357.
