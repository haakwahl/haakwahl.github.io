Biografi

Arn Fredriksson Tungen ble født i 1723 på/i Geitastrand, Orkdal, Sør-Trøndelag. Han giftet seg med Gunhild Persdatter Gjerdet. Han giftet seg med Marit Sjurdsdatter Dyndal i 1755. Han døde i 1787 på/i Geitastrand, Orkdal, Sør-Trøndelag.

Rød, p. 29. and p. 191:
This relationship is somewhat speculative and perhaps shouldn't be credited. This Arn was one of only two Arns of the right age listed in Rød's book on Geitastrand. Rød says that Gunnhild was in service at Tunga. The other possible father of Kjersten is Arn Torsteinsson Meland, b. 1718. He was this Arn's first cousin and was also unmarried at the time of Kjersten's birth. Unlike Rein, Rød documents very few births to unmarried people.
