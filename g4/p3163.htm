Biografi

Doris Evelyn Tharp ble født den 17 februar 1926 på/i Bemidji, Beltrami, Minnesota, USA. Hun giftet seg med Donald Peterson den 12 oktober 1947 på/i Minot, Ward County, North Dakota, USA. Hun døde den 13 februar 2006 på/i Minot, Ward County, North Dakota, USA. Hun ble gravlagt på/i Rosehill Memorial Park, Minot, Ward County, North Dakota, USA.
Doris Evelyn Tharp was also known as Doris Evelyn Tharp Peterson. Hun var utdannet i 1944 på/i Minot High School. Hun var utdannet i 1947 på/i St. Luke's School of Nursing, Fargo, North Dakota, USA.

Over the years she served as nursery room nurse, the head nurse of orthopedics and as an emergency nurse. She also spent 17 years as an instructor at the Trinity Medical Center School of Nursing. In 1984, she was named the North Dakota Nurses Association District Two Nurse of the Year.

After her retirement, she volunteered over 40,000 hours for Trinity Medical Center. She received the Minot Sertoma Club Service to Mankind Award in 1995, was named a Paul Harris Fellow by the Rotary Club of Minot, was past president of the Minot Coterie Women's Federated Club, the Minot Shrine Auxiliary and Trinity Health Auxiliary. She served as president of the Healthcare Auxiliaries of North Dakota from 2000-2002 and was the secretary of that organization. Doris was a member of the women's division of the Minot Country Club, volunteered for the Minot Chapter of the American Red Cross and as a zoo guide for the Roosevelt Park Zoo of Minot. She was also a member of the Sons of Norway.
