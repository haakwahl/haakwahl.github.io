Josene Hannah Krull ble født den 3 april 1927 Live Oak, Sacramento, California, USA. Hun giftet seg med Charles Salinas. Hun giftet seg med Raymond Howard Dougherty. Hun døde den 18 april 2021. Hun ble gravlagt Northern California Veterans Cemetery, Igo, Shasta, California, USA.
Josene Hannah Krull was also known as Salinas. Hun was also known as Dougherty.

Josene graduated from Shasta Union High School in 1944. Her family moved to Chico in 1945 and she graduated from Chico State University in January of 1950, with a teaching credential and a major in business.

Her first teaching position was at Lassen Union High school in Susanville, CA. She also taught at Corning High School, and at Agana High School on the island of Guam.

She was employed at the U.S. Forest Office in Redding for several years and that is where she met her second husband, Raymond Dougherty, whom she called the love of her life.

She was a long time member of American Association of University of Woman (AAUW) and firmly believed in the importance of educating woman. She had many friends and held a variety of offices in that organization.
