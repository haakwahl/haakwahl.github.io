INGVARD LEKNES TAKES OWN LIFE FOLLOWING FALL

The community of Bricelyn was severely shocken friday morning to learn that their esteemed friend and townsman, Ingvard Leknes, had hanged himself in the basement of his home about 8:30 in the morning.

The previous sunday, he had sustained a bad fall in wich he received several bruises, and worst of which was over his eye, very close to the temple, besides beeing rendered unconsciuos for som time and bleeding profusely. Since then he had not been his old self. It is thought that eiter the fall unbalnced his mind or that he became worried about the injuries he had received from the fall and feared that he would suffer from them for a long time, was waht caused him to commit the rash and lamentable act.

The previous day he had taken his revolver down to the local haardware stores and tried to buy bullets for it, but the salesman at both stores suspected a sinister motive rather than the excuse that he wanted to shoot some rats in the store basement, and would not sell him the bullets, so he subsituted the rope.
