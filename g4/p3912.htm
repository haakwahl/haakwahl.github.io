William Richard Reum ble født den 16 juni 1937 Moorhead, Clay County, Minnesota, USA. Han giftet seg med Lois Sanborn i 1960. Han giftet seg med Sharon Gayle Reilly den 20 desember 1973 Palm Beach, Florida. Han døde den 28 september 2016 Plaza, Mountrail County, North Dakota, USA. Han ble gravlagt East Community Cemetery, Plaza, Mountrail County, North Dakota, USA.
William Richard Reum og William Richard Reum. When he was 16, he started racing cars and built his first race car at Reum Garage in Plaza. In 1956, he was the mid-season champion at the NoDak Speedway in Minot.
William entered the US Navy on August 4, 1958 serving until his honorable discharge on November 16, 1960.
Throughout his life, Bill worked at various locations throughout the country including Brow Chevrolet in Massachusetts and Westlie Motor Company in Minot. He was an iron worker, welder and heavy equipment operator helping with the construction of the Garrison Dam, Falkirk Mine and the Pyramid of ND (PAR site) in Nekoma, ND. He also farmed with his brother, owned and operated Bizzy Bill’s Repair and Welding and was a rural US Mail Carrier until he retired.
Bill was a charter member of the NoDak Race Club. He raced car #11 because he was the eleventh charter member. In 2006 he was inducted into the NoDak Speedway Hall of Fame. He enjoyed racing cars and going on numerous fishing and hunting trips with his family and friends. He was a member of First Lutheran Church, the Plaza Volunteer Fire Department, lifetime member of the VFW and served on the Plaza City Council for many years.

Verna Lucille Mork was also known as Verna Lucille Mork Reum. Verna was a police officer’s wife, first in Minot and then for seventeen years, after moving to Plaza in 1955. She was very active in serving her community. She helped establish and maintain the Plaza Museum, was a member of the Cheerio Homemakers and the Plaza PTO. She was a Mountrail County 4-H Leader, served as secretary-treasurer of the Sunday School at First Lutheran Church for many years and served in the ELCW. She diligently wrote the Plaza News for the local newspapers and helped to establish the Plaza Senior Citizens Center.
