Biografi

Arlen Frank Halvorson ble født den 3 november 1942 på/i Rice Lake, Barron County, Wisconsin, USA. Han giftet seg med Connie Brunsell i 1968. Han og Connie Brunsell ble skilt. Han giftet seg med Gay L. Polencheck i 1999. Han døde den 25 juli 2015. Han ble gravlagt på/i Northern Wisconsin Veterans Memorial Cemetery, Spooner, Washburn County, Wisconsin, USA.
When Khrushchev threatened to bury America, Arlen entered the Air Force. He was part of Crash Rescue at the world’s largest fighter trainer base, Luke Air Force Base, near Phoenix, Arizona. At the beginning of the Cuban Crisis, he was awakened at 1 a.m., given a gun, put on a plane and sent to Boca Chica Air Force Base, in Florida. Leaving the heat, his next call was at DEW (Defense Early Warning) Line, in Alaska, at King Salmon Air Force Base. Arlen returned to civilian life and attended Dunwoody Electronic Institute. This led him to a position at Honeywell Research and Development of munitions (electronics) in Minneapolis, Minnesota. He returned to northern Wisconsin to raise his family amidst a small town life. In the 1970’s, he opened Spooner’s first Radio Shack, and owned a TV repair and antennae business. He also worked as a miller at Louisiana Pacific in Hayward, Wisconsin, but it was in 1988 that he found his true calling as a star route mail contract driver with the U.S. Postal Service. Until he retired in June of 2013, Arlen ran a route from Spooner to Birchwood, Hayward, and Ashland and many posts in between, making many people laugh along the way. As a frequent contributor on the radio show “Sound Off with Brad Bennett” on WDSM from Duluth, Minnesota, he found an outlet to share his concerns for politics, America and people. He came close to his dream of opening an antique store in Ironwood, Michigan, when illness re-routed his plans.
