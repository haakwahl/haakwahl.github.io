Biografi

Colin A. Wahl ble født den 12 november 1934 på/i Boyceville, Dunn, Wisconsin, USA. Han giftet seg med Alice D. Greeno den 25 august 1956 på/i Bethany Lutheran Church, New Lisbon, Juneau, Wisconsin, USA. Han døde den 2 april 2008 på/i New Lisbon, Juneau, Wisconsin, USA. Colin døde hjemme. Han ble gravlagt på/i Town of Orange Cemetery, New Lisbon, Juneau, Wisconsin, USA.

When Colin was in the 7th Grade his parents moved from Boyceville to New Lisbon and operated the Wahl Funeral Home.

Colin graduated from the New Lisbon High School in 1952. He served in the US Navy from July of 1952 till Nov. 1955. He later served in the Air National Guard and the US Air Force. He worked in ammunition storage for many years at Volk Field in Camp Douglas, WI.

Colin was an avid hunter, who loved duck hunting. He also enjoyed fishing and trapping, and just being outdoors. He was a member of Ducks Unlimited and of the National Rifle Association.
