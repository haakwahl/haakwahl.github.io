He attended school in Nome and joined the U.S. Army in 1942. He served in the European Theater with the 33rd Calvary, Troop D, 20th Armored Division, during World War II.
He began a career with the S&L Corporation in 1946, managing stores in Valley City, Grand Forks, Slayton, and Ironwood, Mich., before moving to Fergus Falls in 1961. In later years he continued to manage the Bostwicks and Morrie A's stores, retiring in 1986. During his retirement he was employed as a part-time bailiff for the Otter Tail County Court. He also drove bus for Otter Tail Coaches and for high school sports activities. He was a member of First Lutheran Church, was a member and past president of the Lion's Club, the Chamber of Commerce and the Mall Association; a member of Elks, and the VFW. He was an avid supporter of all sports. Norel was a wonderful husband, father, and grandpa. He treasured his family and friends and took special joy in doting over his grandchildren and great-grandchildren.
