Han døde den 3 februar 2006 Bemidji, Beltrami, Minnesota, USA. Han ble gravlagt Sacred Heart Cemetery, Roseau, Roseau County, Minnesota, USA.
Albert was born in a log cabin on his father's homestead in Moose River Township, Marshall County, Minnesota. He was born to Charles and Constance (Aune) Zeidlik. He lived with his family on this homestead until he was five years old, when the family moved to Middle River, Minnesota.

Albert attended the Tin School near Gatzke, Minnesota. After finishing school he worked on the family farm, then spent the summers working on farms in the Tabor area. He worked in Middle River delivering fuel for Standard Oil, until the fall of 1941 when he moved to Minneapolis.

From May of 1942 through October 1945, he proudly served his country in the U.S. Army. He served with the 2487th Quartermaster Truck Company (attached to 9th Air Corps) in the European Theatre of Operations during WWII.

Hannah was bornon her Dad's homestead near Middle River, Minnesota. She lived on the farmstead until 1924 when she moved into Middle River. She lived in Minneapolis, Minnesota a short time before moving to Washington, D.C. when she was 18. She started working as a historian for the Center of Military History, Department of the Army, and remained at the position until she retired at the age of 70 years. Hannah was a highly respected military historian and community volunteer, a terrific friend and a much loved aunt to her nieces and nephews. A quick google search on the internet of Hannah Zeidlik will reveal the many dozens of influntial authors who have recognized her contributions in their prefaces. Hannah was dedicated to volunteering her skills to community organizations from education to theatre to museums.
