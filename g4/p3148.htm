DatterBetty Lavin

Biografi

Louise Mary Jacobson ble født den 16 november 1882 Waterford, Racine County, Wisconsin, USA. Hun giftet seg med James William Lavin den 17 juni 1911 St. Charles Curch, Burlington. Hun døde den 28 februar 1961 Burlington Memorial Hospital, Racine County, Wisconsin, USA.
Louise Mary Jacobson was also known as Louise Mary Jacobson Lavin.

Mrs. Lavin's newspaper career began in 1912 when she wrote the Kansasville news for the Waterford Post in a column called "Kansasville Kernels." She became a correspondent for the Racine Journal in 1925 and remained with the newspaper ever since.

A graduate of Milwaukee State Teachers' College, Mrs. Lavin taught the fourth grade in the Burlington Graded School from 1904 to 1911 when she resigned to marry James W. Lavin. The Lavins owned and operated "The Island" summer resort at Eagle Lake until 1925 when they sold it to his nephews, the Usher brothers, who subdivided it. For five years, from 1930 to 1935, Mrs. Lavin was Dover town treasurer.
