Cynthia Christina Hagemo

Pedigree Link

Biografi

Cynthia Christina Hagemo ble født den 25 februar 1942 på/i Butte, Silver Bow County, Montana, USA. Hun døde den 4 oktober 2007 på/i Missoula, Missoula County, Montana, USA. Hun ble gravlagt på/i Claquato Cemetery, Chehalis, Lewis County, Washington, USA.
Cynthia Christina Hagemo was also known as Cynthia Christina Hagemo Ross. She was the youngest of five children. Cyndi attended Washington, Lincoln and St. Patrick grade schools and was a 1960 graduate of Girls Central High School. She then attended Gonzaga University in Spokane, WA, majoring in Pre-Med. She worked for Mountain Bell Telephone Co. in Great Falls before marrying Bruce Anderson. The couple had three children, Tim, Kimberlee, and Kristine, and settled in Missoula.
When all her children were in school, Cyndi became the manager of Mountain View Floral, when it was in the same building as Squire Simmons & Carr Funeral Home. She married Robert G. "Bob" Ross on January 22, 1984 in Butte. Bob was the manager of the funeral home and the couple worked together in both businesses. While in the floral business Cyndi served on the Montana State Floral Association Board of Directors and was the Montana State Ambassador for Teleflora, a nationwide floral wire service.
In 1989 she and Bob moved to Vancouver, WA. Bob was the manager of Vancouver Funeral Chapel and Cyndi worked as the office manager. In 1990 the couple was transferred to Centralia, WA. Bob was the manager of Sticklin Funeral Chapel in Centralia and Brown Mortuaries in Chehalis and Morton, WA. Cyndi worked with him as the office manager, pre-need funeral counselor and developed the "Aftercare" program for all three mortuaries. She decided to further her career and graduated from Centralia College with an Associate Degree in Business Administration. Having completed her requirements for the Washington State Board, Cyndi received her Funeral Directors license in 1992. She continued furthering her education by attending the Corporate Manager's Training in Cincinnati, OH, and the National Foundation of Women in Funeral Service in Chicago, IL, as well as numerous State Association and Corporate seminars and training sessions. After completing another 180 hours of continuing education in funeral service, she received her C.F.S. P. (Certified Funeral Service Practitioner) certification in 1996 from the Academy of Professional Funeral Practice. She served as an Ambassador for the State of Washington, helping other funeral directors attain that same goal. She held "Life Membership" status in that organization as well. Cyndi also served on several committees including the Education Committee of the Washington State Funeral Directors Association. She is a Past President of the Southwest District of W.S.F.D.A.
Cyndi worked diligently with the Twin Cities Chamber of Commerce, serving on the Board of Directors and also the Ambassador Committee with her husband, Bob. She was a Charter Member and Board member of the Hi-Noon Sertoma Club. She and her husband were members of St. Joseph Catholic Church in Chehalis, WA, where they served on the Liturgy Commission and as Eucharistic Ministers. Cyndi is a Past President of the Providence Centralia Hospital Foundation Board. She served as co-chairwoman of the Hospital Foundation Festival of Trees for several years.
