Ole Mathiassen Solum ble født den 29 februar 1856 på/i Overhalla, Nord-Trøndelag. Han giftet seg med Lotte Margrethe Lorentsdatter den 18 juli 1887 på/i Vikna, Nord-Trøndelag. Ole kom til Vikna i forbinelse med at han skulle være med å sette opp kvernbruk i Austafjord, og bla ansatt som første kværnmand (møllemester) der. Ole Mathiassen Solum emigrerte til USA i 1888 sammen med Lotte Margrethe Lorentsdatter og Edvind Olaus Lorentsen. Mr. Solum had been a resident of Irving for a long time, having settled on a farm which he bought in section 10 about seventeen years ago. Last spring he left here with his family for North Dakota, where he had filed on a homestead in Bowman county. He had been there but a short time when he was taken sick. After being in a hospital at Dickinson, N.D., for some time, he was taken to Luther Hospital in St. Paul. From there he was moved to Willmar about a month ago. All that medical skill could do for him was done, but in vain, and death finally came to release him from his sufferings.
