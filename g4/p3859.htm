Biografi

Dr. Fred Mayor Blix, Jr., ble født den 8 januar 1918 på/i Chicago, Cook County, Illinois, USA. Han giftet seg med Marjorie Marie Durst den 15 mars 1945. Han døde hjemme den 27 desember 2018 Indianapolis, Marion, Indiana, USA. Han ble gravlagt på/i Zionsville Cemetery, Zionsville, Boone, Indiana, USA.
Fred graduated 1935 of Carl Schurz High School in Chicago, IL.
Attended the University of Illinois, where he graduated with a bachelor of arts degree in 1940, then, pursued a degree in medicine, graduating from the University of Illinois Medical School in 1943.

Served as a member of the U.S. Army Medical Corp. during World War II.

He started out as a family doctor in Ladoga, as well as doing anesthesiology at Culver Hospital in Crawfordsville and served as Montgomery County Coroner from 1950-1952.
In 1975, he became the director of the Family Practice Residency Program for St. Vincent's Hospital in Indianapolis. He also served as the Medical Director for the St. Vincent Stress Center, retiring in 1985. He served in several locum tenens positions around the country until the age of 80.
He was active in theater, studied Spanish and loved taking the family on trips with an Airstream trailer throughout the United States and Canada.
