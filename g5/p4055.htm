Don was the son of Harvey and Henrietta Dowd, born October 25, 1935 in Lake Mills Iowa. He was the youngest of 4 siblings. Don is a graduate of McGregor High School, McGregor MN. After graduating, he married and started his 20-year military career, enlisting in the United States Marine Corp in California. During Don's time in the Corp, he traveled overseas to Japan and Okinawa. The first of his four children was born in California. The family moved to Albert Lea where the other 3 children were born and raised. Don worked at Wilson Foods/Farmstead in Albert Lea. He was always busy and a hard worker, working odd jobs like laying sod in the summers. Don was also an active member of the Lutheran Church community. He kept his dancing on wheels roller skating alive from his youth by volunteering with church youth groups to local rinks. Don did all of this while finishing his military career in the Army National Guard 135th Infantry where he was honorably discharged. His stint in the Guards included training in Norway. Once retired, Don spent time collecting donations for the Veteran's Home in Hastings.
Donald is survived by his children: Debbie (Larry) Bergeson, Diane (Jim) Anderson, Dwight Dowd, and Denise (Drake) Dietz.
