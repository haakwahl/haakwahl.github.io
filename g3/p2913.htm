Biografi

Martin Mathison ble født den 30 november 1849 på/i Nittedal, Akershus. Han giftet seg med Marith Torgenson. Han døde den 5 juni 1926 på/i Renville County, Minnesota, USA. Han ble gravlagt på/i Palmyra Cemetery, Hector, Renville County, Minnesota, USA.
Martin immigrated to America when he was seventeen years old, landing in New York. He worked his way west by working on the railroad, first settling in McLeod County. Next he moved to Minneapolis and worked as a chore boy for John Pillsbury and became an America citizen. He homesteaded in Martinsburg Township in 1873 and married Marit Torgenson in 1874. They lived on their farm for the rest of their lives and had eight children. Martin was active in his community, serving as township treasurer, school board member and Palmyra Lutheran Church trustee.
