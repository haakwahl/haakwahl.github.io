Richard Edwin Derauf was also known as "Dick" Derauf.
"Dick" graduated high school from the Breck School in St. Paul, Minn. and continued his education at Gustavus Adolphus College in St. Peter, Minn., majoring in business and accounting. Dick enjoyed serving his customers in the packaging business for the past 53 years and was most recently employed at Dairyland Packaging. Dick was very active in his community and participated in the following organizations: Madison Elks Club, Madison East-Monona Rotary Club, Cherokee Country Club, Lake Wisconsin Country Club, East Side Hockey Association and Madison Speed Skating Club. Dick enjoyed playing golf, traveling, visiting with his customers, attending UW sporting events, and spending time with his grandchildren. He is survived by his wife, Nancy; four children, Liz (Tom) Odgren of Middleton, Rick (Janet) Derauf of Houston, Texas, Peter (Lori) Derauf of Waunakee, and Kathryn (Jeff Olson) Derauf of Sun Prairie; grandchildren, Peter, Elyse, Katie, Brad, Kelly, Haley, Sam, Cora and Luke; brothers, Ben (Mildred) Derauf, and Dr. Don (Alma) Derauf; brother-in-law, Roy (Marie) Peregrine; and many other relatives and friends.
